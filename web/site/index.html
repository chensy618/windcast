What this is

Windcast corrects global and regional forecasts of the 10 m wind for the local terrain. A neural network adds the effect of the terrain at each 1 km cell: height, slope, valleys and shelter from the wind direction. Some models first read the weather pattern on the 0.25° grid with a neural operator (SFNO or FNO).

Two sets of input forecasts are used: the ECMWF models (IFS and AIFS), and four physical models (IFS, GFS, MEPS and AROME-Arctic). Each forecast is the mean of 15 networks; the spread shows how much they disagree.

How good is it

Error of the wind (vector RMSE) at stations the models never saw in training, against the IFS + AIFS mean. Lower is better.
